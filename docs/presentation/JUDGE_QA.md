# Renewable Asset Intelligence (RAI)
## Skeptical Judge Q&A: 20 Defensible, Evidence-Backed Answers

---

### 1. What exactly is novel here?
**Answer:** The novelty is not a new deep-learning classifier or an autonomous agent. The novelty is the **closed-loop decision architecture**: closing the gap between raw telemetry anomalies and defensible human maintenance actions. RAI integrates physics-grounded expectations, atmospheric aerosol attribution, peer cohort ranking, audited academic precedent retrieval, counterfactual techno-economic modeling, and human-governed work order dispatch with strict provenance quarantine.

### 2. Why isn't this just anomaly detection?
**Answer:** Anomaly detection only tells an operator *that* something is abnormal; it cannot tell them *why*, *what to do*, or *how urgently*. In renewable energy, a 20% power deficit can be caused by bearing degradation, dust soiling, utility curtailment, or sensor drift. Anomaly detection alone produces alarm fatigue. RAI attributes the cause against weather and peers, finds historical precedent, quantifies the net benefit of acting now versus waiting, and prepares an actionable work order.

### 3. Why not use an LLM directly on SCADA logs?
**Answer:** Large Language Models are notoriously unreliable at floating-point arithmetic, cannot evaluate temporal physical power curves, and hallucinate under distribution shift. In RAI, **zero numerical math is performed inside an LLM**. All expected power generation, residuals, CAMS aerosol integrals, peer percentiles, and Net Present Value cash flows are computed deterministically in Python/DuckDB before the local AI is invoked.

### 4. How do you prevent hallucinations?
**Answer:** 
1. The local reasoner is provided with a strictly typed, immutable `EvidencePacket`.
2. It operates exclusively through deterministic, read-only tools.
3. If its calibrated confidence falls below our 80% threshold, it is programmatically barred from guessing and must flag `Human Escalation Required`.
4. In our formal benchmark evaluation across Tasks A–G, the unsupported-claim rate was **0.00%**.

### 5. What data in the repository is actually real?
**Answer:** Exactly four datasets are external and real:
1. **CARE Benchmark (Zenodo 10958775):** Multi-year 10-minute SCADA across 36 commercial turbines across Farms A, B, and C.
2. **Kelmarsh Wind Farm (Zenodo 5841834):** 6 commercial 2.05 MW turbines with SCADA and Greenbyte operational event logs.
3. **NREL PVDAQ (OEDI):** 450 real, checksummed daily telemetry files across Systems 34, 1239, 1283, 1430, and 1433.
4. **Historical Case Corpus:** Exactly 14 curated, adjudicated real cases (6 confirmed component failures, plus operational, maintenance, and environmental events) derived from CARE, Kelmarsh, and PVDAQ.
The 42-asset operational fleet (Kutch Wind and Charanka Solar) is generated by our calibrated physics simulator.

### 6. Is this system deployed in live commercial plants?
**Answer:** **No.** RAI is an extensively tested research and hackathon release candidate. There are zero live commercial utility SCADA feeds connected in real-time. We state this explicitly in our README, our claims matrix, and our UI.

### 7. Does it actually predict component remaining useful life (RUL)?
**Answer:** **No.** RAI performs early anomaly detection, atmospheric loss attribution, and operational event association. We do not predict multi-month remaining useful life or exact hours to mechanical destruction. Any claim of universal RUL prediction without run-to-destruction sensor suites would be scientifically dishonest.

### 8. Why does Kelmarsh matter if you don't predict failures on it?
**Answer:** The Kelmarsh dataset provides real, open-source SCADA from 6 commercial turbines paired with Greenbyte operational maintenance logs. It allows us to prove that our anomaly scores statistically associate with real operational shutdown events, and contributes 4 real operational/maintenance-event precedents (protection trips, scheduled maintenance, low-wind standstill — not confirmed mechanical failures) to our k-NN retrieval memory.

### 9. Why is solar failure validation still marked "NOT VALIDATED"?
**Answer:** Because independent scientific integrity matters. In Gate 5.6A/B, we acquired and adjudicated 450 real NREL PVDAQ files. However, independent component-failure event labels were unavailable in the public cohort. An earlier synthetic run that evaluated solar models circularly was caught by our internal audit and formally retracted (`artifacts/evaluation/gate56_invalid_prior_run/`). Gate 5.6C remains an unclosed validation gate, and we do not claim validated solar failure prediction.

### 10. How are the historical cases sourced?
**Answer:** Exactly 14 external records are curated and stored in `rai/memory/real_corpus.py`:
- 8 cases from CARE Benchmark (6 confirmed component failures — gearbox, generator bearing, transformer, hydraulic group, rotor bearing, converter fuse — plus 1 healthy-baseline and 1 human-error maintenance case).
- 4 cases from Kelmarsh Wind Farm (2 protection trips, 1 scheduled maintenance event, 1 low-wind standstill).
- 2 cases from NREL PVDAQ (1 inverter outage, 1 clipping event) — zero of the 2 solar cases is a confirmed equipment failure.
Each case contains preserved dataset provenance, raw sensor trajectories, event classifications, and documented limitations.

### 11. How do you prevent synthetic demo data from contaminating real benchmark evidence?
**Answer:** Through our **Dual-Key Quarantine Gate** in `rai/memory/work_orders.py`. A case can only be promoted into the `EXTERNAL_REAL` retrieval partition if it possesses both `FeedbackProvenance.EXTERNAL_FIELD_OBSERVED` AND `ObservationLevel.FIELD_VERIFIED`. All demo simulations, synthetic tickets, and unverified reports are permanently quarantined as `INTERNAL_SYNTHETIC` (currently 122 records), making benchmark pollution impossible.

### 12. Can the AI control the plant or change setpoints?
**Answer:** **Never.** RAI is architected with strict read-only analytical boundaries. It has zero actuator APIs, zero setpoint modification endpoints, and cannot trip breakers. All operational recommendations are staged as proposals requiring explicit authorization by a licensed human operator.

### 13. Are the economic savings real?
**Answer:** No. They are **modeled counterfactual projections**, not realized historical savings. True realized savings can only be verified through a multi-year randomized control trial (RCT) on commercial fleets. We compute Net Present Value under transparent, user-disclosed tariff (₹4.50/kWh) and inspection cost parameters, and label the result as `Projected Avoidable Exposure (Modelled risk estimate)`.

### 14. Why is local AI important instead of using OpenAI or Claude?
**Answer:** Utility infrastructure is critical national infrastructure and often operates under strict air-gap, NERC-CIP, or enterprise data privacy regulations. Transmission of SCADA and substation operational telemetry to third-party public cloud APIs is frequently prohibited. A quantized local reasoner (Needle 2) running in-process guarantees zero external network leakage.

### 15. How does cross-farm transfer work?
**Answer:** In Gate 5.4, we evaluated all 6 transfer directions between CARE Farms A, B, and C under distribution shift (e.g., Farm B rotor speed 7.98 rpm vs Farm A 11.40 rpm). We proved that **target-normal calibration**—using unlabelled normal SCADA from the target farm to re-center physics residuals—recovers 106.3% of baseline performance without requiring any target-domain fault training.

### 16. What happens when evidence is insufficient or contradictory?
**Answer:** The system abstains. In differential diagnosis (`rai/models/differential_diagnosis.py`), if evidence supports multiple competing hypotheses equally, the verdict returns `COMPETING_HYPOTHESES`. In local AI reasoning, if confidence is below 80%, the agent flags `Human Escalation Required`. Unknown evidence remains explicitly flagged as unknown.

### 17. What is your single strongest scientifically validated result?
**Answer:** Our out-of-sample anomaly detection and cross-farm transfer on the peer-reviewed CARE wind benchmark (Zenodo 10958775): achieving normal classification accuracy > 0.995 and 106.3% recovery under target-normal calibration. (Our internal Gate 2 holdout PR-AUC of 0.822 is a separate, self-graded metric on our own 42-asset fleet, not a CARE-benchmark figure.)

### 18. What would you do next if given access to commercial utility plant data?
**Answer:**
1. Ingest 12 months of high-resolution SCADA and computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) work order logs.
2. Fit site-specific target-normal calibration baselines.
3. Validate true positive and false positive alert rates against actual physical teardown records.
4. Close Gate 5.6C for solar by adjudicating real inverter failure logs.

### 19. What happens if live weather API data is unavailable?
**Answer:** The weather provider degrades gracefully to local cached forecast files in `artifacts/weather_cache/`. If no cache is available, the dispatch optimizer assumes worst-case weather safety constraints and flags operational windows as restricted until updated forecasts are ingested.

### 20. What happens if the local AI model (Needle 2) is unavailable?
**Answer:** The system automatically falls back to our deterministic rule-based decision synthesizer (`rai/agent/fallback.py`). This fallback produces schema-identical structured verdicts, complete financial trade-offs, and work order recommendations using pure Python logic, ensuring the operational loop never breaks.
